Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SuperParty ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1. Personal Information

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google, we collect your email address, name, and profile picture to create and manage your account.
  • Contact Information: Phone numbers dialed or received via the application for VoIP services.

1.2. Audio Data (Call Recordings)

  • Voice Recordings: Calls made through the SuperParty app are recorded for operational purposes, quality assurance, Customer Relationship Management (CRM), and to improve our Artificial Intelligence (AI) models.
  • Notification: We inform users via audio prompts ("This call may be recorded") and in-app notifications that calls are being recorded.

1.3. Usage & Device Data

  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: We collect anonymous data about how you interact with the app (screens visited, session duration) and crash logs to improve stability.
  • Device Identifiers: IP address, device model, and OS version for security and diagnostics.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • Service Provision: To facilitate VoIP calls and connection between employees and clients.
  • Quality Assurance & CRM: Call recordings are analyzed to improve customer service and maintain accurate business records.
  • AI Improvement: De-identified audio data may be used to train and refine our AI algorithms for better call analysis and automation.
  • Security: To detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized access.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We engage trusted third-party service providers to process data on our behalf:

  • Twilio: For VoIP connectivity and temporary call recording processing. (Twilio Privacy Policy)
  • Google Firebase: For authentication, analytics, and crash reporting. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Hetzner: We store call recordings securely on our private servers hosted by Hetzner.

4. Data Retention

We retain personal data and call recordings only as long as necessary:

  • Call Recordings: Generally retained for 30 days, after which they are automatically deleted, unless a longer retention is required for legal compliance or specific CRM cases.
  • Account Data: Retained as long as your account is active.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data.

Requesting Deletion: You can request the deletion of your account and associated data (including call recordings) at any time.

Please visit our Data Deletion Instructions page or contact us at superpartybyai@gmail.com.
