Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with Google Play Data Safety policies and GDPR, SuperParty provides a method for users to request the deletion of their accounts and associated data.

How to Request Deletion

Step 1: Send an Email

Send an email to superpartybyai@gmail.com.

Subject Line: Data Deletion Request - [Your Email/Username]

Step 2: Provide Confirmation

In the body of the email, please include:

  • The email address associated with your SuperParty account.
  • A statement confirming you wish to delete your account and/or call recording data.
  • (Optional) If you only want to delete specific call records, please provide the approximate date and time of the calls.

Step 3: Processing

Our support team will verify your request and process the deletion within 30 days.

Once completed, all data associated with your account (profile, logs, and audio recordings) will be permanently removed from our servers and third-party processors (Twilio/Firebase).
